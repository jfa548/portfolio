The brief

SemiCab runs a collaborative, AI-driven trucking network. Freight is dense and numbers-heavy, and a deck about it can easily drown in statistics. The job was to find the through-line, problem then proof, and give it a bold, confident visual voice that holds attention from the first slide to the last.

What I did

Designed the 17-slide narrative: the problem, the size of the opportunity, the positioning, the proof. Created the "Network Lens" visual metaphor (orange curved paths, white nodes) that threads through the deck. Designed the data storytelling slides, including traditional vs. SemiCab network comparisons. Built a full-page infographic, "From Local Optimisation to Network Efficiency". Set the typographic system: bold orange on black, data treated as a design element.

Opening the problem — truckload transportation is broken. Framing the scale of the opportunity.

The opportunity

Early slides size the prize in numbers that land — 10% lower transportation spend, 70% fewer empty miles, 35% better on-time delivery — then sharpen the positioning: SemiCab isn’t a smarter digital broker, it’s a different model entirely.

Sizing the opportunity, then staking out the positioning.

The deck is anchored by one idea — the “Network Lens.” Orange curved shapes act as dynamic paths and white circles as nodes; flowing compositions suggest the looped, connected network itself. It gives a dense logistics story a single, repeatable visual that balances efficiency with movement.

The core visual metaphor — the "Network Lens" that threads the whole deck. Note: Figures shown (10% lower transportation spend, 70% fewer empty miles, 35% better on-time delivery) are SemiCab's platform claims, not project results — the “Network Lens” that threads the whole deck.
Data storytelling — the network effect, made visual, and the proof.

A full-page infographic carries the core argument on its own: the shift from local optimisation — rate negotiation, lane-by-lane tweaks, TMS execution — up to true network-level planning, drawn as one connected system.

“From Local Optimization to Network Efficiency” — the argument as a single infographic.

Bold orange on black, confident typography, and data treated as a design element rather than a footnote. Each slide makes one point and hands off to the next, so the argument builds instead of piling up.

Result

A pitch narrative that reads with momentum: a complex, collaborative logistics story made scannable, credible and distinctly SemiCab.
