Overview

SenseAI is an AI agent for procurement — a product whose promise is clarity: faster, clearer, more confident buying decisions. The brand had to feel exactly that way, from the mark outward.

Research

I started by looking at the category — Scoutbee, Coupa, Zycus, GEP and others. They lean on futuristic design, iceberg metaphors and abstract AI motifs: powerful, but often distant or overwhelming. SenseAI needed the opposite — friendly, grounded and easy to trust — so the direction became a rounded typeface and a radiant symbol instead.

Competitor landscape — the futuristic, abstract norm SenseAI deliberately moves away from.

The identity

A rounded wordmark paired with a radiant burst — a small flash of clarity that reads as a spark of insight. The type stays approachable; the burst carries the intelligence. Together they anchor a clean, confident blue system.

The wordmark and the standalone burst mark — the intelligence engine at the heart of the product.

Anatomy & construction

The mark is built on a grid: a central star for the spark of insight breaking through complexity, wrapped in a form that signals movement, repetition and computing power. It’s drawn to stay scalable, recognisable and crisp at any size, from a favicon to a hero.

Logo anatomy — the star of insight plus the computing-power form, constructed on a grid.
Scalable across positive, reversed and dark variants.

In use

The identity extends to a hero website — laid out around a clear eyebrow, outcome-led headline and low-friction CTA — and a set of social banners, keeping one burst and one voice across every touchpoint.

Hero anatomy — eyebrow sets context, the heading names three outcomes, the CTA stays low-friction.
The system applied across the website and social banners.

Outcome

A complete brand system — research, mark, construction, website and social — that gives an AI procurement product the clear, confident presence its promise depends on.
