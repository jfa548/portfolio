Sensecloud

Brand identity and web for SenseAI — an AI procurement agent.

Role
Brand & Digital Designer
Client
Sensecloud
Year
2024
Type
Brand & Web

Overview

SenseAI is an AI agent for procurement — a product whose promise is clarity: faster, clearer, more confident buying decisions. The brand had to feel exactly that way, from the mark outward.

The identity

A rounded wordmark paired with a radiant burst symbol — a small flash of clarity that reads as a spark of insight. It works as a logo, a favicon, and an accent, and it anchors a clean, confident blue system.

The SenseAI wordmark and burst symbol.
Brand applied across layouts. System detail and usage.

In use

The identity extends to a hero website and a set of social banners, keeping one burst and one voice across every touchpoint.

Social creative. A second social format.

Outcome

A complete, flexible brand system — mark, website and social — that gives an AI procurement product the clear, confident presence its promise depends on.
