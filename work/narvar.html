The brief

Narvar runs the post-purchase experience (tracking, returns, personalisation) for 1,500+ retail brands. The homepage had to explain an AI-driven platform, prove its scale and win over enterprise buyers, all at once. Every team wanted its product near the top. Without a clear order, the page would say everything and land nothing.

What I did

Redesigned the full homepage end to end, from hero to closing call to action. Set up the page's dark, enterprise-grade visual system: type, spacing and a restrained accent palette. Designed the cinematic AI-led hero, the radial platform diagram, the retail logo wall, the "reward loyalty, reduce risk" value grid, the network stats, the retailer proof wall, the security and press sections, and the closing CTA. Delivered it as a build-ready, responsive system with a reusable hero, platform diagram, stats module and testimonials grid.

Key decisions

1. One promise in the hero, not a feature list.
Buyers decide in seconds whether to keep scrolling. A single, clear promise above the fold gives them a reason to, and pushes the detail into the sections below.

2. The platform as a network, not a list.
Narvar's products (personalisation, fraud prevention, reverse logistics and more) read as clutter when they're stacked. A radial diagram shows them as one connected platform, so the breadth looks like strength instead of noise.

3. Credibility first, then hard proof.
The logo wall sits right under the hero so trust is earned straight away. The page then moves into numbers and retailer stories, taking buyers from promise to proof in a fixed reading order.

Result

The homepage now has a clear reading order and an enterprise feel that matches Narvar's standing. Its components and visual language scale across the rest of the marketing site.
