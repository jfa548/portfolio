Nile Secure

A full campaign suite for a campus-networking startup — concept work for a senior design role.

Role
Senior Designer
Client
Nile Secure
Year
2024–25
Type
Self-Initiated Brief

Overview

A self-initiated campaign suite for Nile's AI-powered campus network, built as concept work ahead of a senior design conversation. It spans a landing page, social creative and display ads, all driving to one asset: the Architecture Blueprint whitepaper.

The system

Deep navy, fibre-optic light and a single hot-pink call to action. The direction is dark and cinematic — “Reimagine your campus network” — and the same visual language holds across every format so the campaign reads as one.

Social creative — campaign key frame. A second social format.
Display and carousel creative. Consistent system across formats.

Outcome

A complete, coherent campaign concept — landing page, display, social and video — that keeps one hot-pink CTA and one cinematic voice across every placement, built to show end-to-end campaign thinking.
