Overview

A self-initiated campaign suite for Nile's AI-powered campus network, built as concept work ahead of a senior design conversation. It spans a landing page, social creative and display ads, all driving to one asset: the Architecture Blueprint whitepaper.

The system

Deep navy, fibre-optic light and a single hot-pink call to action. The direction is dark and cinematic — “Reimagine your campus network” — and the same visual language holds across every format so the campaign reads as one.

Social creative — the campaign key frames.

The landing page

Everything drives to one asset — the Architecture Blueprint whitepaper. The landing page runs from a cinematic hero through a trusted-by logo wall, the “four pillars” benefit grid and a proof section, down to the download form.

The full landing page, top to bottom.

The LinkedIn carousel

A three-beat carousel built for the feed: name the pain, show the shift, make the ask. “Tired of complex campus networks?” → “Imagine a network that runs itself” → “Download the Blueprint.”

The LinkedIn carousel — problem, shift, call to action.

Display ads

The same system, sized for the ad slots that matter — a 300×250 medium rectangle and a 160×600 skyscraper — so the campaign holds up wherever it runs.

Display ads at their true sizes — the 300×250 medium rectangle and the 160×600 skyscraper.

Outcome

A complete, coherent campaign concept — landing page, mobile, LinkedIn carousel and display — that keeps one hot-pink CTA and one cinematic voice across every placement, built to show end-to-end campaign thinking.
