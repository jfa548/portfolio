Overview

Insider helps brands personalise customer experiences. For the beauty and cosmetics vertical, the work spanned two connected pieces: an in-depth digital growth guide, and the identity and promotion for Uplift — the brand's customer-experience event.

The guide

The growth guide combines editorial layout, lifestyle photography art direction and data visualisation into something marketers actually want to read. Statistics, storytelling and product screens sit in one calm system, so dense material stays approachable and on-brand.

Spreads from the guide — personalisation in the beauty industry, and “Meet Insider.”

The event

Uplift 2025 needed an identity that could scale from a stage backdrop to a standee to social. Before any of that, the design had to be grounded in who it was for and how it should feel.

Who it’s for

The audience framed every decision: marketing leaders, e-commerce leaders, CRM & loyalty managers, CX owners, Insider customers and digital-transformation leads — senior people who needed the event to feel credible and energising, not gimmicky.

Target audience — the segments the identity had to speak to.

Type & moodboard

The wordmark came down to a type choice with a clear rationale: Whyte Inktrap — sharp, edgy, creative — against Söhne’s neutral authority. The moodboard then set the warm gradient palette and the logo variations that carry the “next step is up” idea across the system.

Type exploration and moodboard — the rationale behind the wordmark and palette.

The result

From that groundwork, a bold wordmark over fine contour lines in a warm palette scales cleanly from the stage to a standee to a story ad.

Uplift 2025 event standee — the identity scaled to a vertical format.
Event promotion across social formats. Adidas-led social and web display ads on the Insider system.

Outcome

A cohesive editorial and campaign system: a guide that positions Insider as a credible partner for beauty marketers, and an event identity that holds together from the main stage down to a story ad.
