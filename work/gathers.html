Overview

For Narvar's presence at the Gathers event in San Diego, I designed a patch bar — a hands-on merchandise experience where guests built their own keepsake instead of being handed a generic giveaway.

The experience

Two steps, printed on one clear sign. First, pick one of four Narvar logo patches. Then choose from seven San Diego city designs — stamps, a retro badge, a script lockup — applied live to a jacket or bag. The signage had to make that choice obvious at a glance in a busy room.

Four Narvar patch designs for the first pick. Seven San Diego city patches — stamps, badges and a script lockup.

Outcome

A branded, interactive takeaway that turned a booth into a small craft moment — on-brand for Narvar, specific to San Diego, and memorable enough that guests wore it out of the room.
