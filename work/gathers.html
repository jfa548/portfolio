Gathers for Narvar

A custom patch bar that swapped a generic giveaway for a keepsake guests designed themselves, on the spot.

The brief

Narvar was at the Gathers event in San Diego and wanted something better than another branded giveaway that ends up in a drawer. The answer was a patch bar: guests build their own keepsake. It had to be clearly Narvar, feel specific to San Diego, and be simple enough to understand at a glance in a busy room.

What I did

Designed four Narvar logo patches for the first pick. Designed seven San Diego city patches: stamps, a retro badge and a script lockup. Designed the patch bar signage that explains the two-step choice on one clear sign. Created the event branding around the experience.

Key decisions

1. Two steps, one sign.
In a crowded room, nobody reads instructions. Splitting the choice into "pick your Narvar patch, then pick a San Diego patch" and putting both on one sign made it obvious in seconds.

2. Brand first, place second.
The Narvar patch always comes first and has a fixed spot (upper left sleeve), so every finished piece carries the brand. The city patch is the fun, personal part, placed anywhere the guest likes.

3. Local, not generic.
Lighthouses, sailboats, sunshine and palm trees in stamp and badge formats tie the keepsake to this event and this city, which makes it something people want to keep and wear.

Four Narvar patch designs for the first pick. Seven San Diego city patches — stamps, badges and a script lockup.

The wider event identity

The patch bar lived inside a larger piece of work: giving Gathers San Diego its own identity. At the centre was the gathers badge — a circular, stamp-style mark built to live on patches, signage, screens and merch. I explored it widely before narrowing to a system.

Badge directions explored across sunrise, wave, surf and a radiating compass-sun — in a wide range of colourways.
Narrowing the field — the strongest directions tested as real embroidered patches, each with its own palette and board lockup.
The final system — five colourways, each with a sun-and-wave badge, a vertical lockup and a matching seamless pattern.

Result

A branded, interactive takeaway that turned a booth into a small craft moment: on-brand for Narvar and specific to San Diego.
