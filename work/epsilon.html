Overview

A self-initiated brief exploring creative for Epsilon's MarTech platform, worked to the real brand guidelines — Electric Blue and Navy, strict type, a hand-drawn icon system. The centrepiece is TeXpedition '26, a campus hackathon, taken from concept to a full landing page.

The landing page

“Big Idea Energy. Bring It On.” A bold hero sets the tone, then the page carries through an about section, a bento grid of reasons to join, a timeline and a prizes block — a complete, on-brand recruitment page for the event.

Hackathon social creative. Monthly training dashboard email.
About section — introducing the hackathon. Why-join bento grid and rewards.

Outcome

A multi-deliverable brief — hackathon identity, landing page, email templates and campaign analysis — that shows range within tight brand rules, and the discipline to design to someone else's system as cleanly as my own.
